Wedding Photography

Wedding Photographer in Curitiba, Brazil

Fine art, authentic coverage for couples getting married in Curitiba and its metropolitan area.

We're Alline Moltocaro Ribeiro and Quenia Mara Moltocaro, wedding photographers based in Curitiba, Brazil. AQ Studio & Co. was founded in Curitiba, and it's here, in our own city, that we photograph a large share of the love stories we cover every year — long before any destination wedding in Italy or Europe, this has always been our home base.

We work in fine art wedding photography: an editorial, timeless look, with natural direction instead of stiff posing. The goal is always the same — whether the wedding took place in a classic venue in downtown Curitiba, a countryside winery, or an open-air space — capturing the real emotion of the day, without staging it.

How we work

Every Curitiba wedding we photograph goes through three simple steps: an initial conversation to understand your style and day-of plan, technical alignment (timing, light, key moments of the ceremony and reception), and full coverage from the "I do" to the last toast. After the celebration, we handle selection, editing and delivery of the images in a private online gallery, with an optional fine art printed album.

We cover civil, religious, Catholic and symbolic ceremonies in Curitiba, including intimate elopements for couples who prefer a smaller, more personal celebration.

Greater Curitiba area

Beyond the capital, we photograph weddings across Greater Curitiba — including São José dos Pinhais, Pinhais, Colombo, Campo Largo and Araucária — whenever couples want a countryside venue such as a farm, winery or outdoor reception space typical of inland Paraná state.
