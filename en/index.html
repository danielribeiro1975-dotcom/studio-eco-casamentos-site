About Us

Nice to meet you, we're AQ Studio

We're Alline Moltocaro Ribeiro and Quenia Mara Moltocaro, wedding photographers based in Curitiba, Brazil, passionate about telling love stories. We combine fine art photography with careful vendor curation at every destination, so couples can live the experience while we take care of the details.

We photograph weddings in Curitiba and its metropolitan area, and we've also covered weddings in historic settings across Italy, with our partner network across Europe continuing to grow. See our work as wedding photographers in Curitiba.
