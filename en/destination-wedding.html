Destination Wedding

Destination Wedding: Photography Around the World

Based in Curitiba, working worldwide: fine art photography and vendor curation for your wedding in Italy, Europe or anywhere in Brazil.

We're AQ Studio & Co., a fine art photography studio based in Curitiba, Brazil, specializing in destination weddings — weddings held away from the couple's home city. We've photographed weddings in historic settings across Italy and France, and we continue to grow our partner network across Europe and in special locations throughout Brazil.

Beyond photography, we curate trusted vendors at every destination: ceremony venues, catering, décor, music and accommodation, while handling local logistics — so you can experience your wedding, not the planning behind it.

Destinations we cover

Italy

Rome, Tuscany and other historic Italian settings.

Europe

France, Portugal, Greece and other European destinations.

Brazil / Curitiba

From our home base in Curitiba to special locations across Brazil.

How a destination wedding works with AQ Studio

The process starts with a conversation about your dream: style, destination, budget and expectations. From there, we build local vendor curation and the shot list, align every technical detail of the day, and provide full coverage — from the "I do" to the last toast. Afterward, we handle selection, editing and delivery, with an optional fine art printed album.
